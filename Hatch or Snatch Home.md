## Lobby
- **Floating-island underside** (HOS-MAP-002, Matthew): cliff columns under the Halloween lobby, sloping in to a point. See [[HOS-MAP-002 Lobby underside]].
- **Pro altar** (HOS-LOBBY-002, Matthew): altar with a summoning circle in the middle of the plaza (`Workspace.CentralAltar.ProAltar`), rebuildable from `tools/build/HOS-LOBBY-002_pro_altar.lua`. See [[HOS-LOBBY-002 Pro altar]]. Its locked pro gate (tall fading beam wall, PRO LOBBY / LOCKED signs, shown only up close by the client `ProximityFade` controller): [[HOS-LOBBY-003 Pro lobby gate]].
- **Edge walls** (HOS-LOBBY-001, Matthew): invisible walls flush with the island's outer edge (`Workspace.LobbyEdgeWalls`) so players can walk to the edge without falling. See [[HOS-LOBBY-001 Lobby edge walls]].
- **Incubator** (HOS-INC-001, Matthew): animated; walking up opens the incubator screen; each player's egg is 18 cracked puzzle pieces that turn from hologram to solid as it fills (the old faceted egg and core are gone), earned shards fly in, and hatching bursts it. Egg piece meshes are in `ReplicatedStorage.IncubatorEggPieces`. See [[HOS-INC-001 Incubator]]. A camera-facing cosmic egg sign floats above it (`A09_EggSign`, [[HOS-INC-002 Egg sign]]).
- **Ambience** (HOS-LOBBY-004, Matthew): lanterns swing when you're within 60 studs and they're on screen, white wind wisps drift through; every lobby animation (lanterns, wind, incubator, leaderboards, pro gate, weapon pedestals) stops in a match or when the camera is away from the lobby (`LobbyGate`). See [[HOS-LOBBY-004 Lobby ambience]].
- **Jack-o'-lanterns** (HOS-LOBBY-005, Matthew): low-poly mesh pumpkin with a glowing carved face and a warm orange light (kit copy `ServerStorage.HalloweenLobbyKit.A04_JackOLantern`). It replaced the lobby's block pumpkins, and 15 more sit among the trees and graves (`Workspace.LobbyPumpkins`). See [[HOS-LOBBY-005 Jack-o'-lanterns]].
- **Candles** (HOS-LOBBY-006, Matthew): low-poly mesh candles (peach-tinted cream wax with drips, pale neon flame, one yellowish light per group; kit copies `ServerStorage.HalloweenLobbyKit.A05_Candle_*`). They replaced the lobby's 21 block candles, and groups of two or three sit beside the graves that have no pumpkin (`Workspace.LobbyCandles`). See [[HOS-LOBBY-006 Candles]].
- **Rig podiums** (HOS-POD-001, Matthew): four podiums in the incubator pedestal's style (Crown, Obelisk, Rune, Harvest) in `ServerStorage.HOS_RigPodiums`, with a preview row in `Workspace._PodiumPreview`. Not placed in the lobby yet. See [[HOS-POD-001 Rig podiums]].
- **Spin wheel** (HOS-LOBBY-007, Matthew): a Halloween spin wheel, now on the east side of the plaza facing the centre (`Workspace.SpinWheel`). It's made of meshes matched 1:1 to Matthew's concept: skinny stone posts hugging the wheel with hooked lanterns, a studded rim, 8 sectors and a crystal pointer. Generated by `tools/build/HOS-LOBBY-007_spin_wheel_meshes.py` and assembled by its `.lua`. See [[HOS-LOBBY-007 Spin wheel]].
  - **Rewards and spinning** (HOS-SPIN-001): one orange weapon slot (a placeholder until Matthew picks the weapon), plus Gold, Gems and egg shards. Walking up opens the spin screen (`StarterGui.SpinWheelUI`), with a free spin every 15 min, x5/x10 spins and Robux packs (not on sale yet). The server rolls and pays (`SpinWheelService`), and the lobby wheel turns for everyone. Odds and amounts are in `SpinWheelConfig`. See [[HOS-SPIN-001 Spin wheel rewards and screen]].
- **Plaza floor** (HOS-LOBBY-008, Matthew): `Workspace.Ground.Ground_Plaza` is one 82-stud cylinder with the checker texture (`111949839624798`) and studs from the `HOS_Studs` MaterialVariant, replacing 42 strips. See [[HOS-LOBBY-008 Plaza floor]].
- **Halloween sword showcase** (HOS-WEP-002, Matthew): on the Harvest podium each player sees their own avatar holding the All Hallow's Sword, looping idle, jab and lunge (client `WeaponShowcase`, podiums tagged `HOS_WeaponShowcase` with a `ShowcaseSkin` attribute). The lunge only plays in Studio until `LungeStab` is published. See [[HOS-WEP-002 Halloween sword showcase]].
- **Halloween pet showcase** (HOS-HWN-002, Matthew): `Podium_Harvest2` (the east Harvest copy; moved off the Crown 18 Oct) cycles the Halloween shop's pets through idle, move, hop, run and both ability casts with their real effects, under a sign with the name and the current move (client `PetShowcase`, podiums tagged `HOS_PetShowcase`). See [[HOS-HWN-002 Halloween pet showcase]].
- **Emote hologram** (HOS-EMOTE-002, Matthew): the Obelisk podium (north side) shows a floating purple ForceField hologram of you cycling all 51 emotes over a projector light cone. Between emotes it sucks down into the projector and grows back out (client `EmoteShowcase`, podiums tagged `HOS_EmoteShowcase`). See [[HOS-EMOTE-002 Emote hologram showcase]].
- **Showcase shop prompts** (HOS-SHOW-001, Matthew): walking up to the sword, pet or emote podium opens the Shop on Weapons / Featured / Emotes. Close it while still there and a hold-E prompt opens it again (client `ShowcasePrompts`, by the showcase tags). See [[HOS-SHOW-001 Showcase shop prompts]].
- **Leaderboards** (HOS-LB-001, Matthew): the four Halloween boards in `Workspace.Leaderboards` show live this-server stats and turn to face each player. See [[HOS-LB-001 Halloween leaderboards]].
