# Launch and the first week

What to have ready on launch day, and what to read every day afterwards.

## Before you publish

- **Thumbnails:** ten ideas written first, the best two or three made. See the thumbnails guides.
- **Icon:** the strongest thumbnail idea as a 512x512 crop, readable at 50 px.
- **Store page:** name, description, genre, max players, and supported devices (phone, tablet, console). Check each device actually works.
- **Products and passes:** created, priced, and tested through `ProcessReceipt` in test purchase mode. Granted exactly once, surviving a rejoin.
- **Analytics wired in before launch,** not after. Custom events and funnels for the first-time flow: join, first earn, first purchase, first rebirth. Data you didn't collect on day one is gone.
- **Security settings:** enable "Allow HTTP Requests" only if something actually needs it.

## The first week

- **Read the funnel daily.** The biggest drop-off is almost always in the first few minutes. Fix that before adding content.
- **Plan updates from data,** not from what's fun to build next.
- **Write down what changed and its effect** for every update, so the next decision has evidence behind it.
