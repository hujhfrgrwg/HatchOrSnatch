# Turn your game into ten thumbnail ideas

One prompt that turns a description of your game into ten ready-to-generate thumbnail requests, each built on a pattern that already wins clicks.

Thumbnails fail at the idea stage more often than at the image stage. Ten different ideas give the [[Mass-produce thumbnails in Google Flow|Flow batch]] something to choose between, instead of ten versions of one weak picture.

## Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

``` text
You write Roblox thumbnail ideas. Write 10 thumbnail requests for my game, each a different idea.

My game: [genre, what the player does, the most exciting moment, the best reward, any special character or boss]
Title text (if any): "[game name or hook]"

Use a different pattern for each, picking from: before and after progression, tiny vs colossal scale, versus or rivalry, a rare reward reveal, a threat about to hit, a funny reaction, a mid-action peak moment, a collection or rarity wall, an impossible upgrade, a cliffhanger.

Each request is 2 to 4 sentences and says:
- ONE main subject and what it is doing at the peak of the action
- the camera (low angle looking up, high angle, or tilted; never flat at eye level)
- one dominant colour and its opposite as the accent
- one simple background cue
- the title text in quotes and where it goes, or "no text"

Rules: one dominant subject, at most 3 or 4 characters, every flag, building and logo invented. Output only the numbered list.
```

## Then

- Read the list and swap out anything that isn't really your game. A thumbnail that promises a moment the game doesn't have loses the player in the first minute.
- Paste the ten into the [[Mass-produce thumbnails in Google Flow|Flow batch message]].
