# Roblox-legal gameplay trailers

What Roblox allows on a game page video, the specs, and the editing rhythm that keeps a 30-second cut watchable.

## What Roblox allows

Game page videos must be **authentic gameplay**.

- **Allowed:** varied camera angles, minor post-processing, curated highlight sequences, in-game UI text, a game logo overlay.
- **Not allowed:** features the game doesn't have, artificially enhanced graphics, real-life or external footage, text claims or ads ("50% off").
- **Audio:** music from Roblox's catalog or licensed instrumentals. **No voice-over, narration, voice chat, or music with lyrics.**
- Rejected videos still use up your upload quota, so check the rules before uploading.

## Specs

| Item        | Value                                      |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Length      | 30 seconds or less                         |
| Format      | .mp4 or .mov                               |
| Recommended | 1920x1080 at 60 fps                        |
| Maximum     | 4096x2160; width and height divisible by 2 |
| Audio       | 0 or 1 track                               |

Rules and quotas change; confirm them on the Creator Hub before you produce the final cut.

## Editing rhythm

- **Hook in under 3 seconds.** The first frame has to show the game's best idea, not a logo.
- **Speed up travel** to 1.5 to 1.9x so walking doesn't drag, and ease back to 1x as the action lands.
- **Payoffs at 0.5 to 0.6x** for about a second, then back to 1x.
- **Montage beats of 1.2 to 1.6 s,** as hard cuts.
- **One or two whip transitions in total.** More is a slideshow.
- **Title card about 2.5 s,** flashed in.

## Recording

- Record **only the Studio viewport**, cropped to it. Never capture the full screen: whatever else is open ends up in the footage.
- Close heavy apps before recording; dropped frames show as stutter in slow motion.
- Delete test takes and intermediate files as you go.

## Loudness

Normalise the final mix: about **-14 LUFS** with true peak at -1 dB for YouTube and TikTok cuts. Duck the music under sound effects with sidechain compression rather than by hand.

## Social cuts

Cut a 9:16 version for Shorts and TikTok from the same footage. A call-to-action card (about 1.6 s) belongs only in the social cut, never in the Roblox page video.
