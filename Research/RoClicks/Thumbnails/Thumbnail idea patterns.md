# Thumbnail idea patterns

There are 30 reusable ideas here. Each one is a shape that works for any genre: swap in your game's units, pets, cars, machines or monsters. Most strong Roblox thumbnails are one of these patterns.

There are 30 reusable ideas here. Each one is a *shape* that works for any genre: swap in your game's units, pets, cars, machines or monsters. Most strong Roblox thumbnails are one of these patterns.

`<X>` is your game's main thing (a tank, a pet, a car, a brainrot, a machine). Every pattern lists:

- **Shot:** the camera and composition
- **Title:** an example of the text
- **Why it works**
- **Watch for:** the failure we saw with that pattern

## Progression

**1. Evolution lineup.** A row of 4–5 stages, left to right, each bigger and shinier than the last, with a glowing upgrade arrow between each pair.

- Shot: eye level or slightly low, stages on raised platforms.
- Title: `<X> EVOLUTION`
- Why it works: it shows the whole progression curve in one glance.
- Watch for: stages that are the same size. Say "each stage clearly bigger".

**2. Level 1 → Level 999.** Like the lineup but with only the two ends, tiny and pathetic on the left, maxed and glowing on the right.

- Title: `LEVEL 1 → LEVEL 999`
- Why it works: the gap is the hook.

**3. 1 → 1,000,000.** A single lonely `<X>` on an empty baseplate on the left. An endless sea of them filling the right to the horizon.

- Shot: split, with a big arrow between the halves.
- Title: `1 <X> → 1,000,000`
- Why it works: it sells numbers going up.

**4. Max level `<X>`.** One maxed-out hero `<X>` close to the camera, glowing, with a row of weaker versions shrinking behind it.

- Shot: low hero angle.
- Title: `MAX LEVEL <X>`

**5. Every `<X>` unlocked.** An aerial grid of every variant in every colour, like a collection binder.

- Title: `EVERY <X> UNLOCKED`
- Why it works: it shows breadth and rewards collectors.
- Watch for: it fails the squint test if the grid has no hero. Put one gold variant at the centre.

**6. Upgrade in motion.** A column of the same `<X>` that upgrades along its length: rusty near the camera, futuristic in the distance.

- Title: `UPGRADE YOUR <X>`
- Why it works: progression and scale in one image.

**7. Before / after base.** The same plot in two halves: a dirt plot with a tent, and a huge finished base.

- Title: `DAY 1 VS DAY 100`
- Why it works: it is the tycoon fantasy in one frame.

**8. Assembly line.** A factory interior, high three-quarter angle, a conveyor rolling out finished `<X>` with sparks.

- Title: `<X> FACTORY`
- Why it works: it shows the automation loop.

## Contrast and versus

**9. X vs Y split.** A hard vertical divide with two worlds in opposing palettes: calm and warm against dramatic and red.

- Title: `FARM OR FIGHT?` or `BUILD` / `DESTROY`
- Why it works: it shows two play styles and a choice.

**10. \$1 vs \$1,000,000.** A cheap, sad version against a rich, gleaming one, each with a price tag.

- Title: `$1 VS $1,000,000 <X>`
- Watch for: keep the price tags as secondary text, smaller than the title. See `examples/cheap_vs_rich_split.jpg`.

**11. Noob vs Pro.** A default-avatar noob with junk gear against a decked-out pro.

- Title: `NOOB VS PRO`
- Why it works: it is a Roblox-native joke, and every kid gets it.

**12. 1 vs 1,000.** One lone `<X>` in the foreground facing a horizon full of enemies.

- Shot: low, over the hero's shoulder.
- Title: `1 <X> VS 1,000`
- Why it works: underdog tension.

**13. Two sides collide.** Two endless forces meet at the centre, with a glowing gold `VS` and sparks.

- Title: `WHO WINS?`
- Watch for: give the two sides named, opposite colours or they blend into one mass.

**14. Four corners.** Four coloured teams charge in from the four corners toward the centre.

- Title: `4 TEAMS WAR`
- Why it works: it signals multiplayer chaos.

**15. Cheap trick vs real power.** For example, wooden decoys against real units, or a paper boat against a battleship.

- Title: `FAKE VS REAL`

## Scale and spectacle

**16. Giant vs tiny.** A colossal player avatar kneels over a miniature world, placing a tiny `<X>` with two fingers.

- Title: `YOUR TINY <WORLD>`
- Watch for: write "COLOSSAL, 50× taller, ant-sized crowd" or the giant comes out normal-sized.

**17. Endless parade.** A low ground-level shot down a road, columns marching toward the camera to the horizon, a giant invented flag framing one side, and jets with coloured smoke overhead.

- Title: `BUILD AN EMPIRE`
- Why it works: our best performer. It is pure scale.

**18. World's biggest `<X>`.** A wide shot where `<X>` fills the entire baseplate edge to edge.

- Title: `WORLD'S BIGGEST <X>`

**19. Boss `<X>`.** A colossal version towers over tiny players who look up in awe.

- Shot: a very low angle.
- Title: `BOSS <X>`
- Watch for: needs numeric scale words.

**20. Sky full of `<X>`.** The upper two-thirds of the frame packed with flying units, the crowd below looking up.

- Title: `RULE THE SKIES`

**21. Conquer the map.** A high aerial view of territories, where your colour floods over an enemy's tiles with confetti along the edge.

- Title: `CONQUER THE MAP` plus a small badge `8/8`
- Why it works: it shows the meta-goal.

## Reward and rarity

**22. Legendary drop.** A glowing crate or egg falls with a light beam, loot spills out, and characters sprint toward it.

- Title: `LEGENDARY <CRATE>`
- See `examples/reward_crate.jpg`.

**23. Golden everything.** The whole army, farm or pet team rendered in gold.

- Title: `GOLDEN <X>`
- Why it works: rarity fantasy. It reads instantly as "best".

**24. Shocked reaction plus reward.** A player avatar with a jaw-drop face beside an open chest of gems, with a big red arrow pointing at it.

- Title: `FREE <X>?!`
- Watch for: one arrow only.

**25. Secret / mythical tier.** A dark scene with one glowing, rainbow-rimmed `<X>` on a pedestal and silhouetted players gasping.

- Title: `SECRET <X>`

## Threat and tension

**26. Raid incoming.** A cozy base in the foreground at dusk, a red alarm siren, and an enemy force pouring over a hill. The player looks back in panic.

- Title: red banner `RAID INCOMING!` plus a countdown `0:10`
- Why it works: urgency.

**27. Night raid.** A night-vision green tint, searchlights, figures sneaking.

- Title: `NIGHT RAID`
- Why it works: it changes the palette, so it stands out on a bright Discover page.

**28. Defend the base.** The view from behind the defenders on the wall, with the enemy wave coming at the camera.

- Title: `DEFEND THE <BASE>`

## Character and humour

**29. Commander / owner portrait.** One hero character fills 40% of the frame, saluting or grinning, with the whole empire behind.

- Title: `COMMAND AN EMPIRE`
- Why it works: a face sells emotion, and faces read best at small size.

**30. Training montage.** Recruits failing an obstacle course, a shouting drill instructor with a speech bubble of symbols.

- Title: `TRAINING CAMP`
- Why it works: comedy plus an onboarding promise.

## Picking a set

- At least one progression, one versus, one scale, one reward and one threat idea in round 1
- Every idea shows a real mechanic of *your* game. Don't promise bosses you don't have. *Why:* a metadata mismatch gets demoted, and players bounce.
- Each title is 4 words or fewer
- No two ideas in the batch share the same camera and palette. You're testing directions, not variations
- Round 2 spins variations off the round-1 winner only
